✅ Financial Recovery Roadmap

Work these in order. Progress saves privately in this browser only.

    📄 Paperwork Navigator

    Plain-language map of the financial paperwork most divorces require. Information only — not legal advice; rules differ by place.

    Financial Affidavit / Disclosure

    The court's official snapshot of your money: income, expenses, assets, debts. You'll swear it's true — accuracy matters more than optimism. Gather 3–6 months of statements before filling anything in.

    Asset & Debt Worksheet

    Everything either of you owns or owes, usually tagged marital vs separate. Bank accounts, retirement funds, vehicles, house, credit cards, loans. Copy account numbers; never close accounts unilaterally mid-process unless advised.

    Budget / Expenses Form

    Your monthly cost of living — courts use it for support decisions. This is exactly what the calculator above helps you estimate honestly.

    Settlement Agreement

    The contract dividing property, debt and support. Mediators or document services can draft uncontested versions for far less than litigation. Read every number against your worksheets before signing.

    Support Calculations

    Child (and sometimes spousal) support usually follow statutory formulas from both incomes + parenting time. Free calculators exist for most jurisdictions — search "[your location] child support calculator".

    💳 Debt Triage

    Post-split debt is common. Order your payoffs: avalanche (highest rate first — cheapest) or snowball (smallest balance first — most motivating).
